                                  FOREWORD

         The agriculture sector is considered as key in enhancing the economic growth of a country
along with mitigating the concerns of food security. This sector is the main source of rural poverty
alleviation and employment generation. The agriculture sector is contributing 22.7 percent to the
GDP and provides employment to around 37.4 percent of the labour force. It is the main sector for
social formation in our country.

Statistical data and information are crucial in attaining the national development goals and socio-
economic development. Adequate, reliable, and accurate statistical data is required in economic
planning, and analyzing the data facilitates in acquiring results of the present economic status and
also in explicating the future outcomes. It provides the basis for the factors that shape development
planning and decisions at the regional and district levels.

The publication "Crops Area and Production (Districts Wise)" comprises of latest districtwise data
of Major and Minor crops including Food and Cash crops, Pulses and Oilseeds, Fruits, Vegetables,
and Condiments statistics for the years 2020-21 and 2021-22. This publication will prove to be
immensely helpful in facilitating the research work and will help government functionaries,
academicians, and all those who are interested in the issues relating to the development of the
Agriculture Sector.

I wish to appreciate the efforts made by the team who are directly or indirectly involved in the
compilation of this publication, including the federal and provincial organizations, which positively
assisted the Economic Wing to compile the publications.

Suggestions and comments for further improvement of this publication would highly be appreciated.

                                             Executive Summary

         Agriculture is the backbone of Pakistan's economy, contributing around 22.7% to the country's
gross domestic product (GDP) and employing over 37.4% of the workforce. The sector mainly comprises
of crops, livestock, and fisheries, with wheat, cotton, rice, maize and sugarcane being the major crops.
Agriculture is highly dependent on the country's irrigation system, as the majority of the land is arid or
semi-arid. In recent years, the sector has faced numerous challenges, such as water scarcity, climate
change, and low productivity, which have negatively impacted the country's food security and economic
growth. However, the government has implemented various initiatives, such as the Kissan package, to
improve the sector's performance and address these challenges. Despite the obstacles, agriculture remains
a vital sector for Pakistan's development, and the country has the potential to become a major food exporter
in the region with the right policies and investments.

         Agriculture supports industry and earns foreign exchange through exports and reduces poverty at
national level. The development of agriculture depends on sound policies and plan based on the empirical
data. Ministry of National Food Security and Research has prepared its publication "Crops Area and
Production (District wise) 2021-22" to facilitate policy makers, researchers, academia and students dealing
with Agriculture.

         The production of major Kharif crops 2021-22, such as sugarcane, rice, and maize indicated
considerable improvement compared to last year and has surpassed the production targets. The production
of sugarcane production increased from 81.0 million tonnes to 88.7 million tonnes, where major growing
districts of Sugarcane crops are Rahimyar khan, Ghotki, Charsada and Harnai.

         Production of Rice has been increased rice production increased from 8.4 million tonnes to 9.03
million tonnes in 2021-22, and maize production increased from 8.9 million tonnes to 10.6 million tonnes.
Major maize growing districts in Pakistan are Vehari, Swat, Thatta and Chagai.

Wheat:
       Wheat is a major food crop of the country. The wheat area target for the year 2021-22 was fixed at
9.21 million hectares and production target at 28.88 million tonnes. The wheat production during 202122
was estimated at 26.4 million tonnes against 27.5 million tonnes last year. During 2021-22, area sown
decreased to 8.9 million hectares (2.1 percent) against last years of 9.2 million hectares. The production
of wheat declined to 26.4 million tonnes (3.9 percent) compared to 27.5 million tonnes production of last
year. Wheat production deteriorated due to decline in area sown, shortfall in irrigation water and drought
conditions at sowing, less fertilizers off take, though the government has increased Minimum Support
Price to Rs. 2200/40 kg this year.

Rice:

Rice is an important food as well as cash crop. It is the second main staple food crop after wheat and
major export commodity. It contributes 0.5 percent in GDP and 2.4 percent value added in Agriculture.
Out of total production of 9.3 during 2021-22, around 4.9 million tonnes were exported. The production
of Rice during this year estimated at 9.3 million tonnes higher by 10.7 percent than last year's production
of 8.4 million tonnes. The rice area and production target for year 2021-22 were fixed at 3.07 million
hectares and 8.20 million tonnes respectively.

Maize:

Maize is the third important cereal crop of Pakistan after wheat and rice, and it contributes 0.7 percent to
GDP. Maize is cultivated as a multipurpose crop for food, feed and fodder. During 2021-22, maize was
cultivated on an area of 1.65 million hectares showing an increase of 16.6 percent over last year's 1.40
million hectares. Its production increased to 9.5 million tonnes witnessing significant growth of 19.0
percent over 8.9 million tonnes last year. The increase in production was largely due to increase in area,
availability of improved variety of seed, and better economic returns.

Sugarcane:

Sugarcane is a high value cash crop of Pakistan and is of great significance for sugar related industries.
During 2021-22, sugarcane was cropped on 1.26 million hectares recorded an increase of 8.2 percent
compared to last year's sown area of 1.16 million hectares. A bumper sugarcane crop production recorded
at 88.651 million tonnes during 2021-22, up by 9.4 percent over last year (81.009 million tonnes). This
increase was mainly due to favorable weather conditions, better management, and timely availability of
quality inputs.
Cotton:

Cotton crop often referred to as the "white gold" of the country. Cotton is a major source of employment
for millions of farmers and laborers, and it is a key raw material for the country's textile industry and it is
contributes around 0.6 percent to GDP. During 2021-22, the cropped area declined to 1.9 million hectares
(6.8 percent) against last year's 2.07 million hectares. Cotton production increased to 8.329 million bales
(17.9 percent) against last year's 7.064 million bales.
